Connect the ESP32-CAM

The console fetches one JPEG from the bin’s camera and runs OCR and barcode reading in the browser. Flash the standard CameraWebServer example (or any sketch that serves /capture) and use these settings:

  • Open this file from your laptop or phone while on the same Wi-Fi as the ESP32-CAM, then paste the camera’s address on the Scan tab.
  • In the sketch, add the header Access-Control-Allow-Origin: * to the /capture response so the browser may read the image.
  • Mount the camera 12 to 15 cm above a matte, light surface, with an LED strip beside it so glare stays off the foil. Use the highest resolution the board supports (SXGA or UXGA) for small expiry text.
  • Browser OCR needs internet on first use to download the OCR engine and English data.

How the analysis works

  1. Image is converted to high-contrast grayscale and upscaled.
  2. Barcode is read first. GS1 codes on newer packs can carry GTIN, batch and expiry directly.
  3. OCR text is searched for expiry, manufacture date, batch, MRP, strength, and form.
  4. Rules decide the route: expired goes to compliant disposal, sealed with 6 months or more left goes to donation check, anything else is kept as near expiry or normal stock.
